Choosing a stateroom

All six options are balcony or suite staterooms. The honest trade: the three balcony types differ mostly in where they sit and how much room you have inside; the suites add space and amenities at a noticeably higher price.

Ocean View Balcony · 2D

A private balcony overlooking the sea, on decks 6–8. The simplest way to get your own outdoor space on board.

Sunset Balcony · SB

Faces the back of the ship, on decks 6, 8 and 9 — your view is the ship’s wake rather than the side.

Spacious Balcony · 1B

An ocean view balcony with more room inside, on deck 10.

Junior Suite · J4

About 300 sq ft with a bigger balcony and a bathtub — the step up from a balcony room.

Grand Suite · GS

About 400 sq ft, with a bar, a seating area and suite privileges including the Suite Lounge.

Owner’s Suite · OS

About 600 sq ft with a separate living room, dining area and the largest balcony of the six.

Photos are representative; the assigned room’s layout may vary.

How stateroom categories work

You’ll see a short code next to each room in this quote — 2D, SB, 1B, J4, GS, OS. Here’s what those mean, and why a room you find on your own might carry a different one.

The room type stays the same

Royal Caribbean groups staterooms by type — Ocean View Balcony, Spacious Ocean View Balcony, and so on — and then splits each type into several category codes based on where the room sits on the ship. Higher decks, rooms near the middle of the ship and other locations can each have their own code.

The room itself doesn’t change from code to code. A Spacious Ocean View Balcony is the same size, with the same layout and the same balcony, whether it’s coded 1B or another Spacious Balcony code. What changes is the location — and with it, the price, usually by a little.

Why I didn’t quote every code

Most room types on this ship have several codes. Listing every one would mean dozens of near-identical options, so I’ve quoted one clear choice for each type to make comparing easier.

If you find a specific room you like — say a Spacious Ocean View Balcony on a certain deck — it may carry a different code than the one quoted here, and the price may be slightly higher or lower. Send me the room number or the deck you’d prefer and I’ll price that exact room for you.
