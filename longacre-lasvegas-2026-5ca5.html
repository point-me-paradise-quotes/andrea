Fremont Hotel & Casino

200 Fremont St, Downtown Las Vegas · directly on the Fremont Street Experience
~4 mi / 10–15 min to the Strip ~9 mi / 15–20 min to LAS Airport

Fremont sits right under the Fremont Street Experience canopy, so the light show, the casino floor, and every bar and restaurant on the block are a short walk from your room. It's the least polished of the three — older building, straightforward rooms — and that's reflected in the price. A pool is available next door at the California Hotel.

Andrea's Take: if you're spending most of your time out on Fremont Street anyway, you don't need to pay for a fancier lobby. This is the honest-value pick of the three.

Golden Nugget Las Vegas Hotel & Casino

Fremont Street, Downtown Las Vegas · one block from Fremont Hotel
~4 mi / 10–15 min to the Strip ~9 mi / 15–20 min to LAS Airport

The Golden Nugget is the more upscale Downtown play — still steps from Fremont Street, but with multiple restaurants and bars, a spa, and the Tank Pool, where a 200,000-gallon shark aquarium runs straight through the middle of the pool deck. Two room categories are available: the standard Carson Tower and the newly renovated Rush Tower.

Andrea's Take: the Rush Tower is worth the extra roughly $152 for the four nights if you want a room that feels current. Carson Tower is perfectly comfortable, just older finishes.

Circa Resort & Casino (Adults Only, 21+)

Fremont Street at Main, Downtown Las Vegas
~4 mi / 10–15 min to the Strip ~9 mi / 15–20 min to LAS Airport

Circa is the newest and most upscale Downtown property, built adults-only from the ground up. Rooms are contemporary, the casino is large, and the Stadium Swim rooftop pool complex — stacked pool decks facing a giant sports screen — is the property's signature draw. It's considerably more than the Golden Nugget options and pushes past a $1,200 hotel budget, but it's the splurge pick if you want the newest room in the neighborhood.

Andrea's Take: this is the one to book if the room and the pool scene matter more to you than saving money. It's a real step up from the other two, and the price reflects that.
