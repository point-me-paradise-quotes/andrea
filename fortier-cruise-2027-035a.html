Where you'll sleep

Balcony Stateroom

Balcony Stateroom · 2 guests

Representative photo of a Norwegian Viva balcony stateroom.

Your own outside space for the run into port each morning — coffee at the rail while Cozumel or Roatán comes up over the bow, and a quiet place to sit after the pool deck empties out in the evening.

The exact stateroom category, deck and cabin number are assigned at booking. Your advisor will confirm them with you.

A note on location. For convenient access to the outdoor smoking area, I recommend requesting a balcony cabin on Deck 15 or 16 near the forward elevators, subject to availability and confirmation of the smoking area’s location. Smoking is prohibited inside staterooms and on private balconies.

Ports of call

Your itinerary

Times are local and set by Norwegian; they can shift before sailing. Shore excursions are not included in this quote — your advisor can price any you'd like.

  • Day 1 · Sat Feb 13, 2027
    Galveston, Texas
    Depart 4:00 PM
    Board in the afternoon and sail out past the jetties into the Gulf.
  • Day 2 · Sun Feb 14, 2027
    At Sea
    A full day aboard — Valentine's Day, as it happens. A good night to use the specialty dining package.
  • Day 3 · Mon Feb 15, 2027
    Cozumel, Mexico
    8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
    Nine hours ashore. Clear, shallow reef water on the island's west side, and fish tacos and cold Mexican beer in town.
  • Day 4 · Tue Feb 16, 2027
    Roatán, Bay Islands, Honduras
    8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
    Green hills dropping straight into the water. West Bay's beach and the reef just offshore are the usual draw.
  • Day 5 · Wed Feb 17, 2027
    Harvest Caye, Belize
    7:00 AM – 5:00 PM
    Norwegian's own island destination off southern Belize — a beach, a pool and the longest day in port on this sailing.
  • Day 6 · Thu Feb 18, 2027
    Costa Maya, Mexico
    7:00 AM – 1:00 PM
    A morning call — six hours. Worth planning something close to the pier rather than a long drive inland.
  • Day 7 · Fri Feb 19, 2027
    At Sea
    The slow day home. Pack after lunch, then one last sunset from the balcony.
  • Day 8 · Sat Feb 20, 2027
    Galveston, Texas
    Arrive 8:00 AM

Travel documents: requirements for cruises and port calls are set by the authorities and can change. Check travel.state.gov and Norwegian's guidance before you sail, and your advisor will go over what you need.
