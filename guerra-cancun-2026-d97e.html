Full Details — Margaritaville Island Reserve Riviera Cancún

Why I Chose This Resort for Your Family

Margaritaville Island Reserve Riviera Cancún offers a nice balance between a fun family resort and a relaxing tropical getaway. It is smaller and easier to navigate than many of Cancun's large all-inclusive resorts, while still offering plenty for both children and adults.

An additional benefit for your family is that Margaritaville Island Reserve Riviera Cancún is Autism Double-Checked certified, with autism-awareness training and resources designed to help make travel more comfortable for families with autistic travelers.

Your Room

The Paradise Junior Suite offers approximately 463 square feet of space with two queen beds, a sitting area, furnished terrace and a spacious bathroom with a rainfall shower and freestanding tub.

The rooms have a fresh, modern Caribbean feel and provide the updated, comfortable accommodations you are looking for without moving into the ultra-luxury price range.

Food & Restaurants

Since good food is high on your wish list, this was an important consideration when choosing this resort.

Your all-inclusive stay includes meals, snacks and alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages. The resort offers several dining experiences ranging from sit-down restaurants to casual poolside options, snacks and coffee.

Having different styles of dining also gives you flexibility if the 4-year-old isn't in the mood for a longer sit-down meal.

Pools & Activities

The resort has four swimming pools, including two oceanfront pools, along with activities throughout the day.

You'll also find non-motorized water activities such as kayaking and snorkeling.

There is enough going on to keep the kids entertained without the entire resort feeling like a giant water park. Because the property is smaller, it is also relatively easy to return to your room for some downtime when needed.

Something for the Adults & Teenager Too

One reason I like Margaritaville for your family is that it doesn't feel like a resort designed exclusively for younger children.

The adults can enjoy the pools, beach, tropical drinks, spa, fitness center and relaxed resort atmosphere, while your 15-year-old still has pools, water activities, games, music and activities to enjoy.

You do not need to use a kids club or teen club to take advantage of the family activities at the resort.

Evening Entertainment

There are activities and entertainment throughout the week, which may include live music, karaoke, trivia, games, dance activities and other scheduled entertainment.

This gives you options for something to do together after dinner without needing to leave the resort every evening.

Beach

The resort is directly beachfront, so you'll have easy access to the sand and Caribbean throughout your stay.

I do want to be transparent about the beach because I know this is important to you. The beach here is nice, but I would not rank it among the very best beaches in Cancun.

This section of the Riviera Cancun/Puerto Morelos coastline can experience varying water clarity and occasional seaweed. Conditions change throughout the year and even from day to day.

If having the absolute best turquoise-water beach is your highest priority, another resort may be a better match. If you're looking for a good overall combination of beach, pools, food, activities and a more manageable resort environment, Margaritaville has a lot going for it.

Autism & Sensory Considerations

This is one of the reasons Margaritaville stood out to me for your family.

The resort has autism-focused training and resources, and its smaller size can make it easier to navigate than a very large, busy resort.

We can also request a quieter room location away from elevators, entertainment areas and other high-traffic locations. While special requests can never be guaranteed, I think having an easy place to retreat and decompress when needed is an important consideration when choosing your resort.

Airport Transportation

The resort is relatively close to Cancun International Airport, which helps avoid spending a large portion of your arrival and departure days in a vehicle.

I've included private round-trip airport transportation for $109.

Your family will have private transportation directly between Cancun International Airport and the resort rather than using a shared shuttle that may make multiple resort stops.

Overall Fit for Your Family

I think Margaritaville is especially worth considering if your priorities are good food, comfortable updated accommodations, a manageable resort size, pools and activities for the kids, entertainment for your teenager, relaxation for the adults and a shorter airport transfer.

The biggest compromise is the beach. The biggest advantages are the smaller resort atmosphere, autism-friendly features, dining, family/adult balance and convenient location.

Full Details — Catalonia Grand Costa Mujeres All Suites & Spa

Why I Chose This Resort for Your Family

Catalonia Grand Costa Mujeres is a really strong value for Christmas week, especially considering that it is an all-suite resort with a beautiful beachfront location, several dining choices, large pools and activities that appeal to younger children, teenagers and adults.

It has a modern, upscale feel without moving into the ultra-luxury price range. The property has also received a AAA Four Diamond rating, which recognizes its overall level of design, comfort, service and amenities.

Your Room

The Family Suite Superior gives your family more space than a traditional hotel room and is designed specifically with families in mind.

The resort itself is relatively new and features modern suites with a terrace or balcony, so this is a good option if clean, updated accommodations are important to you.

Food & Restaurants

This is one of the reasons Catalonia stands out in its price range.

The resort has a good variety of dining options located throughout the property, including buffet dining, themed à la carte restaurants, casual food courts and other quick options. The central Quinta Avenida area is home to several restaurants, bars and food venues, making it easy to find something different without leaving the resort.

There are choices ranging from international and Mexican cuisine to Asian-inspired dining and more casual meals, which gives you considerably more variety than a smaller all-inclusive resort.

For a family traveling with both a 4-year-old and a 15-year-old, I especially like having casual dining available alongside sit-down restaurants. It gives you flexibility without having to structure every meal around a formal dinner reservation.

Pools & Fun for the 4-Year-Old

Catalonia has two large swimming pools with swim-up bars. One pool is geared more toward activities and family fun, while the other has a more relaxed atmosphere. There is also a children's pool area.

That setup is nice because you aren't limited to one busy main pool. Your family can spend time around the activity pool when everyone wants more going on and move to a calmer area when you want a different atmosphere.

The resort does have children's and teen clubs, but you certainly do not need to use them. There are pools, beach time, games and resort activities that you can enjoy together as a family.

Something for the Adults & 15-Year-Old Too

This is another area where I think Catalonia fits your family well.

The Quinta Avenida area runs through the heart of the resort and includes restaurants, bars, food courts, shops and both daytime and evening activities. This gives your 15-year-old somewhere to walk around and explore with the family while still providing plenty for the adults to enjoy.

The resort offers a combination of active areas and more relaxed spaces, so it doesn't feel like the entire property revolves around small children.

Evening Entertainment & Family Activities

There are daytime and nighttime activities throughout the resort, particularly around Quinta Avenida and the entertainment areas.

This gives you something to do together after dinner without needing to leave the property every night. The exact entertainment schedule changes during the week, but you can expect a mix of resort activities and evening entertainment.

Beach

The beach is one of Catalonia's biggest advantages.

Catalonia describes the resort as being located on one of the best beaches north of Cancun, directly across from Isla Mujeres. Costa Mujeres is known for white sand, clear Caribbean water, relatively shallow water and generally gentler waves.

Of the resorts we're comparing in this price range, I would consider this one of the stronger options if having a pretty, swimmable beach is important to you.

As with any Caribbean beach, daily water and seaweed conditions can change, so I would never guarantee perfect beach conditions. But overall, I like the Costa Mujeres beach location considerably more than many of the resorts farther south along the Riviera Cancun coastline.

Catalonia is a larger and more active resort than Margaritaville, so I would be a little more strategic about room location for your family.

I would request a quieter room away from Quinta Avenida, the entertainment areas and the busiest pool sections so you have an easier place to return to if your 4-year-old needs some downtime.

One benefit is having two different pool environments and a large resort with different areas. You can choose how much activity you want to be around rather than spending the entire day in one high-energy location.

Unlike Margaritaville, this is not the option I would choose specifically for autism-focused certification. Its strengths for your family are the room space, beach, dining variety and ability to choose between active and quieter areas.

Airport Distance

Costa Mujeres is north of Cancun and farther from Cancun International Airport than Margaritaville or the resorts directly in the Hotel Zone.

Cancun International Airport is approximately 30 kilometers from the Costa Mujeres area.

I would plan on approximately 35–45 minutes for a private transfer depending on traffic. It isn't an especially long transfer, but it is one of the trade-offs for getting the nicer Costa Mujeres beach.

Private round-trip airport transportation can be added so your family can travel directly between Cancun International Airport and the resort without making multiple hotel stops.

Overall Fit for Your Family

I think this is a very strong option if your priorities are a nice beach, lots of dining choices, modern accommodations, pools for the kids, activities for your teenager and a resort that still offers relaxing spaces for the adults.

Compared with Margaritaville, Catalonia gives you a better beach, more dining variety and more going on around the resort for your 15-year-old. Margaritaville has the advantage for the shorter airport transfer, smaller resort layout and autism-focused features.

At $3,903.18 for four nights over Christmas, Catalonia Grand Costa Mujeres offers a lot for the price and comes much closer to your original resort budget than some of the higher-end Cancun options.
