What Concierge adds

  • A shoreside team before you sail to help arrange activities and reservations, and dedicated hosts on board for the whole week.
  • Priority check-in at the terminal and priority boarding.
  • The private Concierge Lounge, with food and refreshments through the day and complimentary alcoholic drinks during set evening hours.
  • The private Concierge sun deck.
  • Early help booking the popular extras: specialty dining, spa treatments and Port Adventures. Those still cost extra, and getting a particular time can't be assured.

Andrea's take

The Family Verandah is worth a look for the extra room at a relatively small step up. Concierge is the splurge: personal service, private spaces and help getting the bookings you want.

Reading the room types

What the names actually mean

Inside

No window to the outside. The smallest footprint here at about 169 sq ft, and the lowest starting price in the group it appears in.

Deluxe Oceanview

A large porthole window, so you wake to daylight. No balcony.

Verandah

A private balcony is part of the square footage. Good for slow mornings and sail-away from Port Canaveral.

"Family" in the name

The same category with more floor space (about 19–41 sq ft more on the rooms quoted here) and room for up to 5.

Concierge

Larger rooms plus a service layer: dedicated hosts, a private lounge and sun deck, and priority boarding. Details under Option 1.

Not on offer for this sailing

The Concierge Tomorrow Tower Suite and the 1-Story Royal Suite were showing as unavailable when this was priced.
