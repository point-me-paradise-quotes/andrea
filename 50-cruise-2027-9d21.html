The Case File

Exhibit A: Seven Nights, Four Islands

Leelan turns 50 somewhere between Kona and Kauai, which feels about right. The Pride of America is the only U.S.-flagged ship doing this route, so there's no lost sea day — every morning you wake up somewhere new.

  • Honolulu, Oahu
    Saturday, December 4 · Departs 7:00 PM
    Boarding day. Drop bags, find the balcony, watch Diamond Head shrink behind the wake.
  • Kahului, Maui
    December 5–6 · Two days in port
    A full two days to actually see Maui instead of rushing it — road to Hana, Haleakala sunrise, or just Front Street and shave ice.
  • Hilo, Big Island
    Monday, December 7 · 8:00 AM–6:00 PM
    Volcanoes National Park is close enough for a real visit, not a photo-op version of one.
  • Kona, Big Island
    Tuesday, December 8 · 7:00 AM–5:00 PM
    Coffee farms, snorkeling off the lava rock, or just a slow morning at anchor.
  • Nawiliwili, Kauai
    December 9–10 · Two days in port, plus scenic cruising past the Na Pali Coast on the 10th
    Kauai gets the same two-day treatment as Maui. The Na Pali cruising happens in daylight — worth being on deck for.
  • Honolulu, Oahu
    Saturday, December 11 · Arrives 7:00 AM
    Disembarkation morning. Coffee on the balcony one last time before the gangway.
