Your trip at a glance

Two Mexican ports, two sea days, Thanksgiving at sea

Jewel of the Seas leaves Tampa on Sunday afternoon, spends a full day crossing the Gulf, then puts you ashore in Cozumel and Costa Maya on back-to-back days. Thanksgiving, Thursday November 26, falls on the second sea day — a slow day on the way home rather than a day of travel. The ship is back alongside in Tampa at 7:00 AM on Friday.

With eight of you, this quote is built around two staterooms of four guests each. Both cabins are booked in the same category, so each option below shows the price for one cabin and for the pair.

Step 2 · Your stateroom

Four balcony options

Each option is priced for four guests sharing one stateroom, and the second cabin is the same price in the same category — so the total for both cabins is simply double. Prepaid gratuities are included in every price. Pricing shown is for the occupancy quoted — ask your advisor for other party sizes.

Guarantee or a set category — what's the difference?

With a guaranteed balcony, Royal Caribbean assigns your stateroom. The cabin number, deck and location aren't chosen in advance, and the view may be partly obstructed. Booking a set category (1D, 3B or 1B below) fixes the type of stateroom you're paying for; the exact cabin number is confirmed when you book.

Royal Caribbean prices the same kind of stateroom in several categories, usually by where it sits on the ship. Your advisor can tell you where each one is.

With two cabins booked, there's no assurance they'll be next to or near each other — worth telling us if that matters to you.
