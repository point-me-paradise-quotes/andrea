Courtyard by Marriott Oahu North Shore

7 nights, December 21–28, 2026 · 3 room types · $3,426.04 to $3,627.87

Your choice

The only branded hotel in Laie, beside the Polynesian Cultural Center on Oahu’s windward North Shore, about a 67-minute drive from Honolulu airport. It is a small, low-rise, garden-style hotel: three floors, 144 rooms, a courtyard pool with a firepit and cabanas, and a small white-sand beach across Kamehameha Highway. The whole property was renovated in October 2024. The pool, hot tub, 24-hour fitness center, pickleball, basketball and volleyball are included, and cribs are free on request.

The honest trade. This is a quiet, well-run base rather than a resort. There is one restaurant, The Bistro, and it does not serve alcohol. There is no spa, room service or shuttle, and a car is essential. The hotel lists guests under 14 as not permitted in the pool, hot tub or fitness center, yet it also has a children’s pool, so it is worth confirming the detail with the hotel if you are travelling with younger children.

ʻAlohilani Resort Waikiki Beach

Your choice

A design-forward resort with 839 rooms in two towers, on the Diamond Head end of Kalakaua Avenue. The honest trade. The resort is across Kalakaua Avenue from the beach, about a 2-minute walk. Every standard room is 31 square metres, so what you are choosing between is the view, not the space. There is no traditional room service.
