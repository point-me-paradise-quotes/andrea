Choosing the place

Fishing, excursions and what each destination is known for in February

None of the fishing charters or excursions below are included in the hotel prices unless a hotel's section says so. Fish move with the water and the weather — these are the typical February patterns, not a sure thing. Your advisor can price any of them once you've picked a destination.

Puerto Vallarta, Mexico

Hotels in this quote: Velas Vallarta · Hyatt Ziva

Banderas Bay — a deep, sheltered bay ringed by the Sierra Madre, with a walkable cobblestone old town and the Malecón seawall promenade.

February is the middle of humpback whale season in Banderas Bay (roughly December through March). Mothers and calves come into the bay's warm water, and whale-watching boats run daily — for many families this ends up being the highlight of the trip.

Fishing in February

Offshore and in-bay sportfishing out of Marina Vallarta and La Cruz

  • Dorado (mahi-mahi) — the cooler months are the strong season; they gather around floating debris
  • Striped, blue and black marlin — present through winter, usually farther offshore
  • Sailfish — around year-round, with the biggest numbers in spring
  • Yellowfin tuna — offshore, size varies by year
  • Red snapper and jack crevalle — reliable inshore and near the rocks all year; the easiest bite for kids

Half-day inside-the-bay trips suit kids best; full-day runs to the offshore banks are for the serious anglers.

What families do here

  • Humpback whale watching in the bay
  • Islas Marietas — protected islands with snorkeling and blue-footed boobies; access rules are strict, so book early
  • Los Arcos — snorkeling around granite arches off the south shore
  • Zip lining in the jungle canyons south of town
  • Sayulita — surf town with easy beginner waves, about 50 minutes north
  • The Malecón — evening walk with street food and sculptures

Cancun & Riviera Maya, Mexico

Hotels in this quote: Hotel Xcaret · Finest Playa Mujeres

Turquoise Caribbean water, cenotes (freshwater sinkholes you can swim in), Maya ruins, and the eco-parks around Playa del Carmen.

Winter here is dry season — sunny, with lower humidity than summer. Northerly winds can make some boat days choppy.

Fishing in February

Offshore Caribbean sportfishing out of Isla Mujeres, Cancun and Playa del Carmen

  • Sailfish — the winter run off Isla Mujeres builds from January into spring; February is well into it
  • Wahoo, mahi-mahi and blackfin tuna — offshore alongside the sailfish
  • Kingfish and barracuda — nearer to shore, good action for kids
  • Amberjack — a winter favorite on the reefs

Wind is the deciding factor in February — captains often switch to a half-day near shore when it blows. Finest Playa Mujeres is minutes from the Isla Mujeres boats; fishing is not part of the Xcaret package.

What families do here

  • Xcaret parks — Xplor ziplines and underground rivers, Xel-Há snorkeling (included with Hotel Xcaret)
  • Cenotes — swimming in clear freshwater caverns
  • Tulum ruins — Maya ruins above the beach
  • Chichén Itzá — a long day (about 2.5–3 hours each way from Cancun)
  • Isla Mujeres — catamaran day with snorkeling, golf carts around the island
  • Cozumel reef snorkeling by ferry from Playa del Carmen

Puerto Rico

Hotels in this quote: Hyatt Regency Grand Reserve · San Juan Marriott

A US territory — no passport needed for US citizens, US dollars, US phone plans. Old San Juan's blue cobblestones and 500-year-old forts, El Yunque rainforest, and glowing bio bays.

Your travel dates fall close to a new moon, which is when the bio bay glow shows best — your advisor will confirm the moon phase before booking the tour.

Fishing in February

Inshore lagoon fishing in San Juan, offshore trolling out of Fajardo and San Juan

  • Tarpon — San Juan's lagoons hold tarpon year-round; a big, jumping fish just minutes from Condado
  • Snook — in the same lagoons and mangroves through winter
  • Wahoo and mahi-mahi — offshore, good in winter
  • Sailfish — offshore in winter
  • Blue marlin — mostly a summer and fall fish; February is not their peak

Lagoon tarpon trips run on calm, protected water — a good option for kids prone to seasickness.

What families do here

  • Bioluminescent bay — night kayak at Laguna Grande in Fajardo; every paddle stroke lights up blue-green. No swimming allowed; age rules vary by operator
  • El Yunque rainforest — waterfalls and trails; your advisor will check current access rules
  • Zip lining — Toro Verde in the central mountains runs some of the longest lines anywhere (about 1.5 hours from San Juan; age and weight minimums apply)
  • Old San Juan — El Morro and San Cristóbal forts, colorful streets
  • Icacos catamaran — snorkel and picnic at a deserted cay off Fajardo
  • Luquillo kiosks — a strip of beach food stalls; try alcapurrias and mofongo

Nassau & Paradise Island, Bahamas

Hotels in this quote: Grand Hyatt Baha Mar · The Royal at Atlantis

Very clear, shallow water in every shade of blue, big resort water parks, and day trips to the Exumas.

February is one of the cooler months — warm enough for the beach most days, with water that some kids find brisk. Both Bahamas hotels have heated water-park options.

Fishing in February

Offshore trolling, reef and bottom fishing out of Nassau and the Atlantis marina

  • Wahoo — the winter fish here; the season runs roughly November through February
  • Mahi-mahi — offshore with the wahoo
  • Snapper, grouper, yellowtail and kingfish — reef and bottom fishing in the clear shallows, where you can often see the fish before they bite
  • Barracuda — year-round, great fun on light tackle
  • Bonefish — on the flats, for anyone who wants to try fly fishing

Nassau's fishing grounds start about 15 minutes from the dock, so half-day trips are easy to fit around water-park days.

What families do here

  • Exuma swimming pigs — full-day trip by boat or small plane
  • Blue Lagoon Island — dolphin and sea lion encounters
  • Rose Island — snorkel and beach day
  • Glass-bottom boat and reef snorkel — easy with young kids
  • Aquaventure (included at The Royal) or Baha Bay (included at Grand Hyatt)
  • Arawak Cay Fish Fry — conch salad and fried snapper; Queen's Staircase downtown
