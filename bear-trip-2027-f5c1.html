Why we're recommending the refundable rate for this group

Both resorts have a less expensive non-refundable rate available. For a solo trip, that might make sense. For a group of twenty-plus women booking a year out, it doesn't — and here's the honest math on why.

The non-refundable rate requires full payment up front, with no flexibility if someone's plans change between now and June 2027. If one person has to drop out in April, that room is paid for and nobody gets that money back — including whoever's left holding the room.

The fully refundable rate works differently: it's book now, pay later. You hold your room today with no payment due until May 25, 2027, and you can cancel for a full refund any time before June 4, 2027. If your plans change, you're not stuck, and nobody else in the group is stuck covering for you.

For a group this size, that's the difference between "a few people might have to back out" and "a few people might have to back out and it's actually fine." We'd recommend everyone book the fully refundable rate.

Two resorts, one party

Guests may choose either The Coral or The Royal and still enjoy the main Atlantis amenities together throughout the stay.

What's shared

Both resorts sit on the same Atlantis property, so whoever books where doesn't split the group up. Everyone gets access to Aquaventure, the main pools, the beaches, The Dig and marine habitats, the casino, restaurants, shopping, and the rest of the common resort grounds. There's also a complimentary shuttle running between the hotels, so a Coral guest can wander over to the Royal side to meet everyone, and vice versa. You are not stuck near whichever building your room happens to be in.

The one exception

The Cove and The Reef side of the property is walled off from this — the Cove Pool is reserved for adult Cove guests, and Cascades Pool is shared between Cove and Reef guests only. Neither resort option below is on that side, so it doesn't change anything for this group, but it's worth knowing that staying at the Royal doesn't unlock those specific pools either.
