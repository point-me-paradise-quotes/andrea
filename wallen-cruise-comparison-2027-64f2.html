Sailing A — South Pacific to Hawaii

Norwegian Spirit · 12 nights · June 17–29, 2027 · Papeete, Tahiti → Honolulu, Hawaii

Four French Polynesian islands — Tahiti, Moorea, Raiatea, and Bora Bora — followed by five sea days and a run through Hawaii that includes an overnight in Kauai. It's a long sail, and the two regions feel genuinely different from the rail: volcanic peaks and lagoons early on, then the greener, cooler air of the Hawaiian islands by the second week.

Itinerary

  • June 17 — Papeete, Tahiti
  • June 18 — Moorea
  • June 19 — Raiatea
  • June 20 — Bora Bora
  • June 21–25 — At Sea
  • June 26 — Hilo, Hawaii
  • June 27 — Kauai (overnight)
  • June 28 — Kauai
  • June 29 — Honolulu

I'd personally lean toward Free at Sea on a cruise this long — for roughly $35 per person per day more, you're covering drinks, specialty dining, Wi-Fi, and the shore excursion credit.

Sailing B — Aruba, Bonaire & Curaçao

Celebrity Beyond · 8 nights · June 5–13, 2027 · Roundtrip Miami

All three ABC islands on one sailing, with four sea days built in for pool time and slower mornings. Aruba gets the longest port day at 15 hours, so there's real time to get off the ship and explore beyond the pier.

Itinerary

  • June 5 — Miami, Florida
  • June 6–7 — At Sea
  • June 8 — Aruba, 8:00 AM–11:00 PM
  • June 9 — Bonaire, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM
  • June 10 — Curaçao, 8:00 AM–4:00 PM
  • June 11–12 — At Sea
  • June 13 — Miami, arrive 7:00 AM

On an 8-night sailing, All Included works out to about $75 more per person per day. If you know you'll be drinking through the week and want Wi-Fi sorted, it can make onboard budgeting a lot simpler.

Sailing C — Hawaii Inter-Island

Norwegian Pride of America · 7 nights · June 19–26, 2027 · Roundtrip Honolulu

This one covers four Hawaiian islands without a single flight or hotel change in between. You get a full day and a half in Maui, a stop each in Hilo and Kona on the Big Island, two days in Kauai, and scenic cruising along the Na Pali Coast on the way out.

Itinerary

  • June 19 — Honolulu, Oahu
  • June 20–21 — Maui
  • June 22 — Hilo, Big Island, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM
  • June 23 — Kona, Big Island, 7:00 AM–5:30 PM
  • June 24–25 — Kauai, with scenic cruising along the Na Pali Coast
  • June 26 — Honolulu, arrive 7:00 AM

At about $37 per person per day more, Free at Sea holds up well here if you plan on drinks, specialty dining, Wi-Fi, and Norwegian's shore excursions. We can also tack on a pre- or post-cruise stay in Hawaii if you'd like more time on Oahu or another island.

Sailing D — Voyage of the Glaciers, Grand Adventure

Discovery Princess · 14 nights · July 24–August 7, 2027 · Roundtrip Vancouver

Two full weeks in Alaska, with Glacier Bay visited twice plus College Fjord and Hubbard Glacier. Late July into early August is when salmon runs are active in a lot of areas, which tends to bring bears in closer to rivers and streams, and it's also solid timing for whale activity — with the long daylight hours Alaska is known for in summer. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed, but the timing works in your favor here compared with earlier sailings.

Itinerary

  • July 24 — Vancouver, depart 4:00 PM
  • July 25 — At Sea
  • July 26 — Ketchikan, 6:00 AM–2:00 PM
  • July 27 — Juneau, 8:00 AM–9:15 PM
  • July 28 — Skagway, 7:00 AM–8:30 PM
  • July 29 — Glacier Bay National Park, scenic cruising
  • July 30 — College Fjord, scenic cruising
  • July 31 — Anchorage/Whittier, 12:30 AM–8:30 PM
  • August 1 — Hubbard Glacier, scenic cruising
  • August 2 — Glacier Bay National Park, scenic cruising
  • August 3 — Icy Strait Point, 6:00 AM–7:00 PM
  • August 4 — Juneau, 6:30 AM–4:15 PM
  • August 5 — Ketchikan, 10:00 AM–5:00 PM
  • August 6 — At Sea
  • August 7 — Vancouver, arrive 7:30 AM

Of everything we've compared, this is the one I'd point you toward first if wildlife is a priority — the late-July start lines up with active salmon runs and whale activity, and two full weeks gets you Glacier Bay twice, plus Hubbard Glacier, College Fjord, and Icy Strait Point, without repacking or changing hotels along the way. Premier makes the most sense on a 14-night sailing given the unlimited dining, drinks, Wi-Fi, and included gratuities.

Sailing E — Western Caribbean from Galveston

Royal Caribbean Symphony of the Seas · 8 nights · June 5–13, 2027 · Roundtrip Galveston

This one sails right out of Galveston, so there's no flight to book or airport morning to plan around. The itinerary covers Cozumel, Perfect Day at CocoCay (Royal Caribbean's private island), and Nassau, with four sea days worked in around them. Symphony is one of the larger ships in the fleet, with neighborhoods like Central Park and the Boardwalk, multiple pools, waterslides, and enough onboard variety that a sea day doesn't feel like a day off from the trip.

Itinerary

  • June 5 — Galveston, depart 4:00 PM
  • June 6 — Cruising
  • June 7 — Cozumel, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM
  • June 8 — Cruising
  • June 9 — Perfect Day at CocoCay, 7:00 AM–5:00 PM
  • June 10 — Nassau, 7:30 AM–5:30 PM
  • June 11–12 — Cruising
  • June 13 — Galveston, arrive 7:00 AM

Payment schedule on this one runs a little differently — the $500 deposit (due within 3 days of placing your room on hold) and $3,632.40 final payment (due March 7, 2027) cover fare and taxes; the $296 in prepaid gratuities is billed separately. Worth asking about if you'd rather have it all rolled into one schedule. And if skipping the airport altogether appeals to you, this is the sailing for that.
