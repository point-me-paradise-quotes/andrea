Late May or July?

Expect heat and humidity in both windows at all three resorts. The Atlantic hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30, so July falls inside it and late May falls just before. Seaweed (sargassum) can reach both Mexican Caribbean and Dominican beaches in the warmer months; how much varies year to year and beach to beach.

On price, the windows don't move the same way: Hotel Xcaret Mexico is $517 lower in July than in May, while every Moon Palace room priced here runs $463–$581 higher in July.

Passport and entry rules differ between Mexico and the Dominican Republic and do change. Check each country's page at travel.state.gov before booking, and your advisor will walk through it with you.

Option one

Hotel Xcaret Mexico

All Parks / All Fun Inclusive · Playa del Carmen, Riviera Maya · 5 nights

Around the resort

137 photos · swipe to browse

A beachfront resort right next to Xcaret Eco Theme Park, built as five Mayan-themed casas named for fire, water, wind, earth and the spiral. There's a rooftop infinity pool with a see-through glass swim tunnel, 15 outdoor pools, and 20 restaurants — from Caribbean plates on the sand at La Trajinera to Japanese fine dining at Xin Gao.

The rate reaches past the room: round-trip airport transfers for four, and park access for four each day. Your advisor will confirm exactly which parks that covers.

The honest trade: the highest price of the four rooms here, in both windows — but the only one with airport transfers and park access built in. Quinta Avenida is an 11-minute drive, so nights in town mean a car.

  • Club Xiquillos (ages 4–12) and La Paxanguería, an evening lounge just for teens

  • Whether taxes are inside this price, and Wi-Fi: your advisor will confirm

Option two

Moon Palace The Grand Cancun

All-inclusive · Private beach south of Cancun · 5 nights

Around the resort

182 photos · swipe to browse

A large resort — 1,304 rooms — on a private beach about 16 minutes from Cancun airport. The outdoor water park is the centre of gravity: twisty slides, a lazy river, splash pads and a wave simulator. The Playroom kids' club (ages 4–17) spreads across three floors with bumper cars, neon mini-golf, a laser maze and a small movie theatre.

Twelve restaurants, including Tavola (Italian), Habibi (Lebanese), Jade (Asian) and JC Steakhouse. Taxes and gratuities are included in the rate, and each room carries a USD 500 resort credit — your advisor will confirm what it can be used for.

The honest trade: construction is underway at a neighboring building and the resort notes there may be noise. Airport transfers are extra.

Option three

Moon Palace The Grand Punta Cana

All-inclusive · Private beach, Punta Cana, Dominican Republic · 5 nights

Around the resort

144 photos · swipe to browse

The newest resort here, opened in 2026 on a private beach about 34 minutes from Punta Cana airport. Nineteen restaurants — Casa Macao, JC Prime Room 84 for steaks, Nikos by Athinagoras for Greek, Carvao for Brazilian, Habibi for Middle Eastern — plus 9 pools, a lazy river, a waterslide and a water park.

The free kids' club runs supervised activities through the day. O Spa has 50 treatment rooms, but guests under 18 aren't allowed in, so spa time is adults-only. Rates as priced include taxes and fees, and gratuities are included.

The honest trade: being brand new means fresh rooms and fewer guest reviews to go on. Transfers are extra, and the drive from the airport is the longer of the two times we have.
