Itinerary

Galveston, two Mexican ports, two days at sea

Both sailings run the same route: out of Galveston, a day each in Costa Maya and Cozumel, and two sea days on the ship. Your advisor will confirm port order and times for your sailing.

Costa Maya

A purpose-built cruise pier on Mexico's southern Caribbean coast, next to the small fishing town of Mahahual — a sandy beachfront strip with grilled fish and cold coconut water a short walk or taxi from the ship. The Chacchoben Maya ruins sit inland for families who want a half-day out. Excursions not included

Cozumel

An island off the Yucatán coast whose clear, calm western side is known for snorkeling over reefs. The town of San Miguel is easy to walk, with fish tacos and fresh-cut fruit near the waterfront. Excursions not included

Two sea days on Liberty of the Seas

Liberty is a large, family-friendly ship with an indoor main street, the Royal Promenade, running down its middle. Activities like the FlowRider surf simulator, rock-climbing wall and Royal Caribbean's Adventure Ocean kids program have been part of the ship — your advisor can confirm what's running for your dates and anything that carries an extra charge.
