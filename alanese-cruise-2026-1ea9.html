Sailing A

Liberty of the Seas

Saturday, June 12 – Thursday, June 17, 2027 · 5 nights · round trip from Galveston

Liberty is the shorter, simpler week: two Mexican ports, two sea days, and home Thursday morning. Its spine is the Royal Promenade, an indoor street of cafés and shops running down the middle of the ship. Several interior rooms have windows that look onto it rather than the sea.

Sailing B

Symphony of the Seas

Sunday, June 27 – Saturday, July 3, 2027 · 6 nights · round trip from Galveston

Symphony adds a night and a third port, Roatán, in Honduras’ Bay Islands. It is a much bigger ship, laid out in neighborhoods — Central Park, a planted open-air garden mid-ship, and the Boardwalk at the stern — so the ship itself fills more of the week. The honest trade: more to do on board, more people doing it.

Choosing a room type

Interior

No window to the sea. Dark when the lights are off, which some people sleep better in. On a week where most daylight hours are spent ashore or on deck, it is the straightforward way to keep the cost down.

Oceanview

A window onto the sea — morning light and a look at the port as you pull in. On these sailings it adds $81.00 (Sailing A) or $472.00 (Sailing B) for two guests over the interior room priced here.

Two guests or four

Four sharing one room costs less per person, but it is one room: one bathroom, close quarters, and beds that convert at night. Your advisor will confirm the exact bed layout for four before anything is booked.

Onboard credit

Each stateroom comes with $50 of onboard credit through our group amenity — one $50 credit per room, whether two or four guests are in it.
