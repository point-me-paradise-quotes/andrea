Andrea's Take

For the lowest price with real upside, I'd point you to the Ocean View Guarantee — it's only $60 more than the Interior room, and gets you natural light and a sea view.

If you want a balcony, my pick is the Spacious Infinite Ocean View Balcony. It's only $60 more than the Balcony Guarantee, and unlike the guarantee category, you know exactly what you're getting — an ocean-facing Infinite Balcony, not whatever Royal Caribbean happens to assign.

If a true walk-out balcony matters to you, the Ocean View Large Balcony Stateroom is the one to book.
