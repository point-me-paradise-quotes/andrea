Two oceanview balcony categories for this sailing

A guaranteed oceanview balcony and an oceanview large balcony are both available — here's the difference.

Guaranteed Oceanview Balcony: Royal Caribbean confirms you into the oceanview balcony category, but doesn't assign a specific room number until closer to sailing. You're guaranteed the category, not the exact location on the ship. It can work in your favor — occasional upgrades happen — but it also means the assigned room could carry a partially obstructed view. Neither outcome is something we or Royal Caribbean can predict in advance.

Oceanview Large Balcony: a specific, larger-balcony room in the oceanview category — assigned now, not at sail time, so there's no guarantee-category uncertainty and no obstruction risk tied to this booking.
