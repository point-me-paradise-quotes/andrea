Getting Around

The hotel sits in Condado, close enough to walk to restaurants, shops, and the beach without needing a car. Old San Juan is about a 10–15 minute drive away for the day you want cobblestone streets and the fort.

From Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport (SJU), it's roughly 6–7 miles — about 15–20 minutes depending on traffic. Taxi or rideshare typically runs $20–$30 each way, plus tip. A private transfer can also be arranged on request — just let us know.
