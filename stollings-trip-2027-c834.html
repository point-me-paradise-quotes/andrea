Option 2 · St. Thomas

Buoy Haus Beach Resort, Autograph Collection

Rebuilt in 2023, 94 rooms, a minute from Morningstar Beach
4 nights · Mon June 28 – Fri July 2, 2027 · Marriott Autograph Collection

The newest-feeling option on St. Thomas: the whole property was remodelled in 2023, and every room has Egyptian cotton sheets, a rainfall shower and a furnished balcony. Frenchman's Bay and Morningstar Beach are a one-minute walk; Charlotte Amalie's harbour is four minutes by car.

For a birthday dinner, Isla Blue does oceanview seafood (dinner only, book ahead). Salt Shack is the opposite mood — on the sand, communal picnic tables, wood-fired food, Baja tacos and live music. The Heavenly Spa runs 9,500 sq ft with couples massages and a 10-person infrared sauna, and the Tides infinity pool has private cabanas.

  • Spread across 5 three-storey buildings with no elevators
  • Minimum check-in age 21 · Check-in 4:00 PM, check-out 11:00 AM
  • Credit cards only at the hotel — no cash

The honest trade: the rate covers the room and nothing else. The $95 nightly resort fee names only fitness-centre access plus unspecified "additional inclusions" (your advisor will find out what those are), and breakfast runs about $38 per person.

Option 3 · Aruba

Manchebo Beach Resort & Spa

72 rooms, low-rise, right on the sand
4 nights · Mon June 28 – Fri July 2, 2027 · 4-star class

Small and quiet by Aruba standards — 72 rooms in two-storey buildings, aimed at adults even though it isn't adults-only. Beach cabanas, loungers, umbrellas and towels are free, Eagle Beach is an 11-minute walk, and the airport is 10 minutes away.

Spa del Sol does treatments in five open-air cabanas on the beach. For dinner there's The Chophouse for steak, Omakase Sushi Bar, and Ike's Bistro by the pool for Mediterranean; lunch is at Pega Pega on the beach. There's a free manager's cocktail reception each week.

  • Built in 1966, no elevators — upper floors by stairs only
  • Minimum check-in age 21 · Check-in 3:00 PM, check-out noon
  • Free self-parking (limited spaces)

The honest trade: this is the lowest estimate on the page as a room-only rate, but meals are extra (breakfast is about $45 per person). The all-inclusive rate closes most of that gap.

  • Not includedMeals on the room-only rate
  • All-inclusive rate adds à la carte meals, snacks and drinks, plus room service during set hours; some restrictions apply

Option 4 · Aruba

JOIA Aruba by Iberostar

All suites, opened in 2024, with a rooftop pool for over-16s
4 nights · Mon June 28 – Fri July 2, 2027 · Luxury class

The newest property in this quote, and every room is a suite. Eagle Beach is a 4-minute walk, staff set out chairs and umbrellas, and the infinity pools look over the beach. The rooftop pool is for guests 16 and up, with submerged loungers and poolside butler service; next to it, Zima is an adults-only rooftop bar with DJ sets.

SPA Sensations has a hydrotherapy circuit and seven relaxation areas, and can do massages on the sand. Dining is at Marea, Bucatini and Gaia House of Grill (select nights).

  • Check-in 4:00 PM, check-out noon · Minimum check-in age 18

The honest trade: some JOIA suites have a private hot tub on the balcony — this one isn't listed with one. Ask if that matters to you.

Option 5 · Nassau, Bahamas

Sandals Royal Bahamian

All-inclusive, couples only, on Cable Beach
4 nights · Mon June 28 – Fri July 2, 2027 · All-inclusive · Adults only (couples)

Close to everything is in the price here. Meals, drinks (minibar included), 24-hour room service, tips and the airport transfer are all in the price, and so are kayaks, sailing, snorkelling and windsurfing off the beach. Seven pools, two tennis courts, and the airport is 8 minutes away.

Restaurants include Butch's Island Chop House, Kimonos for Asian, La Plume for French, Tesoro for Italian and Aralia House for seafood at lunch. The Red Lane Spa has a second location on Barefoot Cay, a small island just offshore.

  • Check-in 3:00 PM, check-out 11:00 AM · Minimum age 18
  • Credit cards only at the hotel — no cash

The honest trade: one of the higher estimates on the page, and the earliest cancellation deadline of the six (May 13). Spa treatments and scuba aren't listed as included.

Option 6 · Nassau, Bahamas

Warwick Paradise Island

All-inclusive, adults only, facing Nassau Harbour
4 nights · Mon June 28 – Fri July 2, 2027 · All-inclusive · Adults only · 4-star class

On the harbour side of Paradise Island, with the Atlantis marina a 6-minute walk away and Cabbage Beach 12 minutes on foot. Bikes are free, which makes both easy. Five restaurants: The Verandah by the pool, Edgewater Grill for seafood, Abbiocco for Italian (reservations needed), Tings on a Stick and Chickcharnies Pizzeria.

Amber Spa will send a therapist to your room, or do massages out at the Mermaid's Lookout pavilion over the harbour.

  • No elevators
  • Check-in 4:00 PM, check-out 11:00 AM
  • Credit cards only at the hotel — no cash
  • Guests rate this room 8.2/10 across 143 reviews

The honest trade: the lowest all-inclusive estimate on the page, but add the $240 resort fee paid at the hotel. Your advisor is confirming whether alcoholic drinks are part of the package.
