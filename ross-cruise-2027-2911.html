One thing worth knowing ahead of time: Royal Caribbean doesn't work the way some other lines do when it comes to drink and dining packages — you can't add them at the time of booking. Those get purchased separately, closer to your sail date, once they're released for your sailing. We'll flag it and help you pick the right one when the time comes.

What's included, what isn't

Your cruise fare covers the stateroom, meals in the main dining room and Windjammer, and access to the ship's included entertainment and pools. Prepaid gratuities, if you keep them included above, cover standard stateroom and dining service tipping for all four guests.
