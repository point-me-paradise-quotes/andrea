Every price shown is a real, bookable rate as of today, not a placeholder.

Look through the comparison table and the details on each sailing, then use the form at the bottom to tell us which one your family is leaning toward. Once we hear back from everyone, we'll lock in the group's pick and move into the actual booking process.

A note on the prices below: every price shown is the lowest available rate — an interior stateroom for two — meant as a starting point for comparing sailings against each other. Once the family lands on a cruise, we'll share the full range of room types (ocean view, balcony, suite) and pricing for each, so everyone can pick what fits their group and budget.

A note on deposits and pricing: deposits shown are per cabin, not per person. Current fares, deposit amounts, and any onboard promotions come from each cruise line and reflect today's rates — the cruise lines set those windows, not us, so if a particular promotion closes before the family decides, that sailing will simply be repriced at whatever the cruise line is offering at the time you're ready to book. Once the group narrows down to one sailing, we'll follow up with full pricing broken out by number of guests per cabin (1, 2, 3, and 4), including any occupancy limits or child pricing differences.

Royal Caribbean · Icon of the Seas · Interior Stateroom
