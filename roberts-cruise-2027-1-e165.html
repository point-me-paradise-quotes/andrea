Ports of call

Your itinerary

Shore excursions Not included — you can book them separately or explore on your own.

  1. Day 1Sun, May 16

    Galveston, Texas

    Depart 4:00 p.m.

    Board in the afternoon, find the two balconies, and open the door between them. Sail-away out of Galveston Bay comes with the late-afternoon light.

  2. Day 2Mon, May 17

    Day at sea

    The first full day aboard is the one to learn the ship — Central Park’s open-air gardens, the Boardwalk at the stern, the FlowRider surf simulator and the Ultimate Abyss slide. Some activities and venues carry an extra charge; your advisor can tell you which.

  3. Day 3Tue, May 18

    Costa Maya, Mexico

    11:59 a.m. – 7:00 p.m. · about 7 hours ashore

    A cruise port on the quiet southern coast of Quintana Roo, near the fishing village of Mahahual and its seafront walk. Inland, the Maya ruins at Chacchoben sit in the jungle. The honest trade: a midday arrival, so the morning is yours on board.

  4. Day 4Wed, May 19

    Roatán, Honduras

    7:00 a.m. – 3:00 p.m. · about 8 hours ashore

    The largest of Honduras’s Bay Islands, ringed by the Mesoamerican Reef. West Bay’s white sand and clear, shallow water are the usual draw for snorkelers. An early start and a mid-afternoon all-aboard — plan the day around both.

  5. Day 5Thu, May 20

    Cozumel, Mexico

    7:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m. · about 10 hours ashore

    The longest port day of the trip. Reef snorkeling and diving off the island’s west coast, or the waterfront in San Miguel for tacos al pastor and a walk along the malecón.

  6. Day 6Fri, May 21

    Day at sea

    A slower day heading home across the Gulf — a good evening to use the specialty dining experience if your booking qualifies.

  7. Day 7Sat, May 22

    Galveston, Texas

    Arrive 7:00 a.m.

    Early arrival and disembarkation. Allow time to clear the ship and the terminal before you head home.
