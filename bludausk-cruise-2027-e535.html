Which ship suits three kids under six?

With three children all under six, the questions that matter are: can each child join the kids' club, where can they splash, and what will fill a sea day. That last one counts most on the Royal Caribbean sailings, where two of the four or five days are at sea. Both ships handle little ones well, but in different ways.

Carnival Horizon · June sailings

The Dr. Seuss ship

  • Kids' club from age 2: Camp Ocean puts 2- to 5-year-olds together as "Penguins," with crafts, singing, dancing and story time. Children don't need to be potty-trained, and the youth staff will change diapers if you supply them.
  • Water play: Dr. Seuss WaterWorks on Deck 12 has a kiddie splash zone full of water-spray toys, plus a giant tipping bucket shaped like the Cat's hat. As with most cruise pools and splash areas, only toilet-trained children can use it, so no diapers or swim diapers.
  • Seuss all over the ship: Dr. Seuss Bookville is a reading and play room for quiet time and crafts. There's also the Green Eggs and Ham Breakfast, the Seuss-a-palooza Parade and Story Time.
  • Evenings: late-night Night Owls childcare is available for a fee, so parents can have a grown-up dinner.
  • Your room: Family Harbor rooms come with access to the Family Harbor Lounge. Carnival lists breakfast, snacks, board games and family movies there. Andrea will confirm the current Family Harbor perks.
Liberty of the Seas · July sailings

The newly updated ship

  • Kids' club from age 3, potty-trained: Royal Caribbean's Adventure Ocean is free for ages 3 to 11. To join the 3-to-5 group, a child must be fully potty-trained, with no pull-ups or diapers, and able to use the toilet without help.
  • Under 3: the Royal Babies & Tots nursery takes children from 6 to 36 months, for an extra charge.
  • Water play, diapers included: Liberty is one of the Royal Caribbean ships with a Baby Splash area, built for children still in diapers. Royal Caribbean doesn't allow diapers in the regular pools.
  • Freshly updated: Royal Caribbean finished a major upgrade of Liberty in 2026. It included a redesigned Adventure Ocean, with a separate space for the youngest group.
  • Your room: the Spacious Interior has no window, which makes it easy to get a dark room for naps after a morning ashore.
The honest trade: if any of the three is under 3 or still in diapers, Carnival lets them join the kids' club, and Liberty has the diaper-friendly splash area. If all three are potty-trained and at least 3, both ships work. Then it comes down to Seuss on Carnival in June with Progreso added, or a newly updated ship in July for less time aboard.

Kids' club hours, age groups and any extra charges are set by each cruise line and can change. Andrea will confirm the details for your sailing.
