Where you'll stop

Three ports and four sea days

Nassau

The Bahamas' capital. The pier sits a short walk from Bay Street and the straw market; for lunch, the Fish Fry at Arawak Cay is a quick taxi ride west — conch salad chopped to order.

RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay

Carnival's private island — a beach day with very little to plan. Cabanas and some activities are an extra charge.

Celebration Key

Carnival's own port destination on Grand Bahama, opened in 2025 — beaches and pools close to the ship. Some areas carry an extra charge.

Four sea days

On a reunion, these are the point: long breakfasts, the pool deck, cousins catching up, and nobody racing back to the ship.

Gratuities: prepay or pay onboard?

Carnival's gratuities for this sailing come to $136 per person — $272 for two, $408 for three, $544 for four. You can fold them into your cruise payments now, or have them added to your onboard account during the cruise. Same amount either way; prepaying just means one less thing on your onboard bill. Nothing is charged through this page.

Pick your dinner style

This is for the main dining room, where dinner is part of your cruise fare. Carnival sets the exact times and confirms your seating; the times below are typical, not fixed.
